Saelvo

Daily sales tracker for small shops.

Saelvo helps a shopkeeper record the day's sale in seconds, see the month at a glance, and keep track of who owes what — without paperwork and without a monthly fee.

What it does

  • Daily sales entries — date, sale amount, opening and closing times, and break times.
  • Monthly reports — total sales, daily average, work hours and an estimated profit summary.
  • Udhar (credit) ledger — customers, balances, amounts received or given, due dates and reminders.
  • Optional Google backup — sign in with Google and the app keeps a "Saelvo Sales" spreadsheet in your own Google Drive, so your records survive a phone change.

Your data stays yours

Sales and Udhar records live on your phone. If you enable Google backup, they go only to the spreadsheet in your own Google Drive — never to the developer, never sold, never shared. There are no ads and no usage tracking.
