Saelvo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Saelvo is a daily sales tracker for small shops. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your business data belongs to you. It stays on your phone and, if you choose to sign in, in your own Google Drive. We never receive it, sell it, or share it.

Information the app stores on your device

  • Sales entries — the dates and sale amounts you type in.
  • Udhar (credit) records — customer names and balances you add.
  • App settings — your preferences such as profit percentage, monthly target, theme and reminder time.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it (unless you exported a backup or enabled sync, as below).

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you tap "Sign in with Google", the app asks Google for permission to use Google Sheets and Google Drive file access. With your permission, the app:

  • Reads your Google account email address so it can show which account is signed in.
  • Creates one spreadsheet ("Saelvo Sales") in your own Google Drive and keeps your sales entries backed up in it.
  • Can only see and modify files that the app itself created — it cannot access your other Drive files.

Your sign-in tokens are stored only on your device. No sales data is ever sent to the developer or to any server other than your own Google account. You can sign out at any time in the app's Sync screen, and you can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Deleting the "Saelvo Sales" spreadsheet from your Drive removes the synced copy permanently.

What the app does NOT do

  • No advertising, no ads SDKs.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, messages, location or microphone. Customer names in Udhar records are typed in by you and stay on your device; they are never synced.

Backups you create

The app lets you export reports (PDF/images) and manual backup files. Where those files go afterwards (for example, if you share them over WhatsApp) is under your control.

Children

Saelvo is a business tool intended for shopkeepers and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy
